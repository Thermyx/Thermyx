## 2. Device safety

The insole heats and cools, and carries a battery.

- Follow all assembly, fitting, and charging instructions. Do not modify the
  thermal cassette, bypass the thermal cutoff, or alter the wiring.
- Do not cut outside the marked trim lines. The protected no-cut zone contains
  wiring, sensors, ducts, and the thermal path.
- Stop using the insole immediately and disconnect the battery if you notice
  discomfort, unusual heat, redness, blistering, smoke, swelling, or damage.
- Inspect your feet regularly, particularly if you have reduced sensation.
- Do not use the insole while it is charging, when it is wet or damaged, or on
  broken skin.
- Keep the battery away from heat, do not puncture it, and use only the supplied
  or specified charging arrangement.

The app applies software limits, including locking heating out when its risk
engine escalates. **These are a second layer of protection, not the primary
one.** The insole's independent thermal cutoff is the primary protection, and
neither replaces your own judgement.
